## 1. Account Eligibility & Registration

- You must be at least 18 years of age (or have valid legal guardian consent) and reside in India to register an account.
- Registration requires verifying a valid Indian mobile phone number (`+91`) via One-Time Password (OTP).
- You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your mobile session and PIN credentials.

---

## 2. Autonomous Self-Checkout & Anti-Theft Exit Pass Verification

- Zippyra enables self-service item scanning and digital payment in partner retail stores.
- **Exit Verification Gate**: Upon completing an order payment, Zippyra issues a single-use, time-bound **Exit Pass QR Code**. You must scan this QR code at the physical store exit gate or present it to exit security staff.
- Unscanned items or attempts to bypass exit pass verification constitute unauthorized removal of merchandise and will result in immediate account suspension, store ban, and reporting to law enforcement authorities.

---

## 3. Payments, Billing & Tax Invoices

- All payments (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking) are authorized securely via our PCI-DSS certified gateway partners (*Razorpay / Cashfree*).
- Zippyra generates a statutory **GST Tax Invoice** for every completed purchase.
- For e-invoice enabled stores, invoices carry an official Government Invoice Reference Number (IRN) and scannable IRP QR code.

---

## 4. Cancellation & Returns Policy

- Orders once completed and verified at the exit gate cannot be cancelled.
- Eligible items may be returned within **24 hours** of purchase via the in-app "Request Return" feature, subject to staff inspection.

## 5. Account Deactivation & Data Erasure

- You may deactivate your account or request complete personal data deletion anytime under **Profile -> Privacy Settings** in the app, in accordance with the DPDP Act 2023.
- Data erasure permanently removes your account, saved preferences, and customer profile across all services. Tax invoice records are retained for statutory periods as required by Indian tax laws.
